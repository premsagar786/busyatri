---
name: College transport product
description: The product goals and role boundaries for the college bus-tracking app.
---

**Product rule:** Students track college buses from their stops toward campus. Drivers share their phone GPS without bus-mounted IoT hardware and can post campus parking remarks. Students can view bus locations and remarks; administrators manage drivers, buses, and location records.

**Why:** The user identified these as the product's main purpose and role boundaries.

**How to apply:** Keep driver phone GPS as the live-location source, make parking notes easy for students to find, and keep fleet management in the admin experience.
